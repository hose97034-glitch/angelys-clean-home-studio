# Angelys Clean Home Studio

Build an ultra-premium, luxury cleaning service website for **Angely González Clean Services**, based on the uploaded banner and branding.

**Brand Identity**
- Brand: Angely González Clean Services
- Tagline: “Clean Homes, Happy Days”
- Supporting line: “Clean Spaces • Fresh Living • Lasting Impressions”
- Phone: 786-402-1347
- Services: Residential Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, Move-In/Move-Out, Post-Construction, Carpet & Upholstery.
- Use the logo's elegant forest green, rich gold, warm ivory and white palette throughout.

**Design Direction**
Create an exceptionally luxurious, expensive-looking website with a unique editorial aesthetic. The hero/banner must be visually stunning, with sophisticated typography, elegant composition, premium cleaning/property imagery, subtle gold details, refined animations and strong visual hierarchy. Avoid generic templates and repetitive layouts. Make every section feel professionally art-directed, spacious and polished.

**Required Sections**
1. Luxury Hero / Banner with premium CTA buttons and phone number.
2. About the Company.
3. Services with elegant image-based layouts.
4. Why Choose Us.
5. Premium Cleaning Gallery.
6. Before & After Showcase.
7. **Reels Section:** Exactly 3 beautiful, empty vertical video placeholders (9:16), ready for my own videos.
8. Testimonials using the supplied glass testimonial component.
9. How It Works.
10. **Service Area Map:** Must include a real interactive map section. Do not omit it or invent an address.
11. Contact / Booking with clickable phone number.
12. Elegant Footer.

**Integrate My Two Supplied Components**
- `feature-carousel.tsx`: Integrate the supplied feature carousel as a premium visual showcase. Preserve its carousel functionality, responsive behavior and elegant transitions.
- `glass-testimonial-swiper.tsx`: Integrate the supplied draggable glass testimonial stack into the testimonials section. Adapt its styling to the forest-green, gold and ivory brand palette. Do not use the demo's fake customer names or fabricated reviews; use editable placeholders until real testimonials are provided.

Follow the supplied component code and create the required files inside `/components/ui`. Install missing dependencies and fix all imports, TypeScript errors and component compatibility issues. Use the existing shadcn, Tailwind and TypeScript structure.

**Important Requirements**
- Make the entire website fully responsive.
- Use premium, high-quality cleaning and luxury-home imagery.
- Add smooth scroll reveals, subtle hover effects and refined transitions.
- Keep all business information easy to edit.
- Use only the supplied business details; never invent reviews, ratings, addresses or business claims.
- Keep exactly 3 Reels placeholders.
- Ensure the Map section is visibly included.
- Do not create unnecessary sections or redesign the brand identity.
- Make the final result look like a custom-designed luxury cleaning company website, not an ordinary cleaning template.
